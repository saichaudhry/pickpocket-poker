Pickpocket Poker

    How to play
    • Goal: best poker hand wins the pot, but go over 21 and you bust.
    • Card values: 2–10 as shown, J/Q/K = 10, A = 11 or 1.
    • You start with 2 hidden cards. Everyone antes into the pot.
    • Each turn pick one: Draw (face-up card from the deck), Steal (take another player's face-up card; they draw a replacement), or Stand.
    • Every card you draw is face-up, so it can be stolen from you.
    • Max 5 cards. Straights, flushes and full houses need all 5.
    • After 4 turns, hands are revealed. Ties split the pot; if everyone busts, the pot carries over.
    • The ante doubles every 3 rounds. Last player with chips wins.
